Conference Overview

About the Conference & Objectives

The International Conference on Computing & Mathematical Frontiers 2027 (ICCMF 2027), organized by the Department of Computer Applications (AICTE Approved), Shrimati Indira Gandhi College, brings together global researchers, academicians, industry practitioners, and students onto a premier interdisciplinary forum.

Centered on the theme “Pioneering Intelligent Computing and Advanced Computational Mathematics for a Quantum & Autonomous Future,” the conference fosters collaborative dialogue across Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Algorithms, Stochastic Modeling, Optimization, Data Analytics, and Cloud-Edge Technologies.

About College

Empowering Women Through Quality Education Since 1984

Shrimati Indira Gandhi College is a self-financing Arts & Science College for Women, Affiliated to Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli. Established in 1984, the college focuses on empowering women through quality education, skill development, and economic independence. Recognized by UGC under section 2(f) and 12(b), the college has been accredited with an “A” grade by NAAC (4th cycle).

Call For Papers

Conference Tracks & Research Domains

Original research papers and abstracts are invited from academicians, research scholars, students, and industry experts across the following four frontier tracks.

  • Deep Learning & Neural Architectures
  • Generative AI & Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision & Pattern Recognition
  • Explainable & Ethical AI Systems
  • Quantum Algorithms & Quantum Machine Learning
  • Post-Quantum Cryptography & Security
  • Autonomous Robotics, Drones & Self-Driving Tech
  • Cyber-Physical Systems & Swarm Intelligence
  • Numerical Analysis & Differential Equations
  • Graph Theory, Combinatorics & Discrete Math
  • Stochastic Processes & Fuzzy Logic
  • Operations Research & Metaheuristic Optimization
  • Big Data Engineering & Predictive Analytics
  • Cloud-Edge Continuum & Serverless Systems
  • 5G/6G Wireless, IoT & Smart Infrastructure
  • Blockchain & Cybersecurity Protocols
Submission Guidelines

Author Instructions & Deadlines

  • 1
    Abstract Submission (By 30 Dec 2026) Submit an original structured abstract (250–300 words) clearly stating objectives, methodology, and key findings.
  • 2
    Full Paper Submission (By 6 Jan 2027) Full-length manuscripts (4–6 pages) must follow standard double-column IEEE formatting with similarity below 15%.
  • 3
    Paper Submission Fee: Rs. 500/- Registration is mandatory for all participants and authors. All presented papers receive official conference certificates.
